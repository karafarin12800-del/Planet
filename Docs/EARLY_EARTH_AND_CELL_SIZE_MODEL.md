# Planet Genome — Early-Earth and Cell-Size Model Notes

**Status:** evidence-informed educational model; not a validated abiogenesis or genotype-to-phenotype simulator.

## What the evidence supports

- Earth formed about 4.54 billion years ago. Evidence for ancient life is indirect and debated in parts of the oldest rock record; the exact pathway and location of life's origin remain unresolved.
- Candidate environments include submarine hydrothermal systems, volcanic settings, and shallow-water or wet–dry environments. These are competing or potentially complementary hypotheses, not a single established answer.
- Prebiotic chemistry requires plausible feedstocks, liquid water or other suitable solvent conditions, and energy. Experiments and geochemical models investigate parts of the pathway; they do not establish a complete, agreed sequence from geochemistry to the first cell.
- The earliest life known from the geological record was microbial. The exact ancestor, morphology, metabolism, and genome of the first life are not known.
- Cell size varies by taxon, strain, growth phase, nutrient availability, and environment. A genome sequence alone does not give a defensible exact cell-size measurement without a calibrated, validated taxon-specific model or direct measurement.

## How the current app should describe itself

1. The environment selector offers *illustrative scenarios* (hydrothermal vent mixing zone, volcanic pond, shallow pool, mixed). Its parameter values are model inputs, not reconstructed global averages for early Earth.
2. The selector changes the digital ocean habitat conditions used by the ecology model. It does **not** simulate a full geochemical reaction network or prove that life emerges from non-life.
3. Digital lineages currently use a synthetic 168-bp genome with eight operational modules. These are not real genes or validated biological functions.
4. A digital organism's visual dimensions are for display only. The app must show its actual biological cell size as **unknown / not measured** unless a taxon-specific source or measurement with provenance is attached.
5. Imported real genomes can support sequence statistics and source annotations. A reference sequence or accession alone does not prove a particular phenotype, cell size, metabolism, or morphology.
6. Evolutionary outcomes in this model are hypotheses generated by simplified rules, not forecasts of Earth's actual history.

## Recommended evidence labels

- **Observed / measured:** a value from a named, traceable source, with taxon/strain, method, units, and conditions.
- **Source annotation:** a database's recorded feature or function; retain the database/version and do not treat it as independently verified.
- **Model parameter:** a chosen input used to explore scenarios.
- **Model output:** a result generated by the rules and parameters.
- **Hypothesis / unresolved:** plausible but not established as the historical pathway.
- **Unknown:** no reliable value is available for the particular digital lineage.

## Cell-size policy

Do not calculate cell size from the eight synthetic modules. For a real taxon, show a cited range only when a source supports the named species/strain and state the growth conditions if available. Otherwise report “unknown / not measured”. Keep illustrative canvas scale separate from micrometre units.

## Limitations and next scientific work

To move beyond this educational model, implement a versioned prebiotic chemistry model with explicit species/reaction networks, mass and charge balance, temperature/pH/salinity dependencies, uncertainty ranges, sensitivity analysis, and literature-backed parameter provenance. A protocell-transition model would need independently justified rules for compartment formation, replication, heritable variation, and selection. It must not label a simulated transition as the known historical event.

For organism-scale outputs, integrate taxon-specific validated phenotype data and measured size ranges. For metabolism, use high-quality annotations and genome-scale metabolic models where available; do not infer a complete phenotype from genome length, GC content, or a short synthetic proxy.
